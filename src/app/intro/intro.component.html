Partner with us

The Molecule Maker Lab Institute (MMLI) is looking for researchers to join our mission of democratizing molecule-making, offering access to cutting-edge molecule discovery and synthesis platforms, along with a network of expert researchers to drive innovation forward.

Explore a Defined Chemical Space

If your research involves compiling and analyzing a defined chemical space, uploading your blocks to DMM can transform it into a powerful resource—empowering students, scientists, and citizens to uncover new insights and drive discoveries inspired by your work.

Expand on a Building Block

If your research involves searching for new combinations of molecules, uploading your blocks to the DMM can help accelerate your progress—exploring more possibilities and making predictions to identify the promising candidates before synthesis.

AI and Chemistry

In this lesson, students explore the connection between molecular structure, color, and function using AI tools like the Digital Molecule Maker. They investigate how molecules absorb and reflect light, how this property can be manipulated for real-world applications, and how AI can assist in molecular design.

Addressing Global Issues

In this lesson, students explore how organic photovoltaics (OPVs) can expand the possibilities of solar energy, comparing them to traditional silicon-based solar cells. They also examine decision-making strategies in scientific research and apply their findings through hands-on experimentation with organic solar cells.
